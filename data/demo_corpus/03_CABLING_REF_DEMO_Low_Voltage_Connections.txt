SIMULATED / DEMO REFERENCE — NOT OFFICIAL BIS TEXT
CABLING-REF (DEMO) — Household Appliance Low-Voltage Connections

IMPORTANT NOTICE:
This document is simulated reference material for a prototype demonstration.
It is NOT an official BIS document, not a substitute for a wiring code, and
must not be used as verified regulatory or certification guidance.

Clause 1 — Purpose
1.1 This reference provides fictional example clauses concerning internal
low-voltage connections in household appliances.

Clause 3.1 — Conductor Identification
3.1.1 Conductors shall be identifiable during assembly and maintenance.
3.1.2 Protective-earth conductors shall be distinguished from current-carrying
conductors by an appropriate identification method.

Clause 4.2 — Conductor Sizing
4.2.1 The conductor cross-sectional area shall be selected according to the
expected operating current and thermal conditions.
4.2.2 Connections shall not be operated beyond their simulated rated current.

Clause 5.1 — Terminal Connections
5.1.1 Terminals shall securely retain conductors under normal vibration and
handling.
5.1.2 Stranded conductors shall be secured so that individual strands cannot
cause an unintended short circuit.

Clause 6.3 — Strain Relief
6.3.1 Flexible supply cords shall include suitable strain relief where the
cord enters the appliance enclosure.

Clause 7.1 — Insulation Clearance
7.1.1 Conductors shall be positioned to reduce the possibility of contact
between live conductors and accessible conductive parts.

Clause 8.4 — Connection Inspection
8.4.1 Assembly inspection shall verify conductor routing, terminal security,
and visible insulation condition before final enclosure closure.
