SIMULATED / DEMO STANDARD — NOT OFFICIAL BIS TEXT
IS 302-KETTLE (DEMO) — Household Electric Kettles — Specific Safety Requirements

IMPORTANT NOTICE:
This is simulated text created only for a prototype demonstration.
It is NOT official BIS text, has not been verified against a BIS standard,
and must not be presented as a real certification or compliance requirement.

Clause 1 — Scope
1.1 This simulated document provides example requirements for electric
kettles intended for heating water in household use.

Clause 4.1 — Rated Capacity
4.1.1 The maximum recommended water capacity shall be permanently indicated
on the kettle or in its operating instructions.

Clause 5.2 — Water-Level Marking
5.2.1 Minimum and maximum filling levels shall be clearly identifiable.

Clause 6.1 — Lid and Opening
6.1.1 The lid shall remain adequately retained during normal pouring.
6.1.2 The design shall reduce the likelihood of accidental opening while
the kettle is being handled.

Clause 7.3 — Automatic Switching
7.3.1 The kettle shall provide an automatic switching function intended to
stop normal heating when the water reaches the end of the heating cycle.

Clause 8.2 — Dry-Boil Protection
8.2.1 A simulated protective function shall reduce continued heating when
the kettle is operated with insufficient water.

Clause 9.1 — Handle
9.1.1 The handle shall permit normal handling without requiring contact with
hot accessible surfaces.

Clause 10.4 — Steam
10.4.1 Steam discharge shall be directed so that normal operation does not
unreasonably expose the user to hot steam.

Clause 12.1 — Stability
12.1.1 The kettle and its base shall remain stable during normal placement
and removal.

Clause 14.2 — Cleaning
14.2.1 Instructions shall describe safe cleaning and shall warn against
immersing electrical components where applicable.
