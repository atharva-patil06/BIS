SIMULATED / DEMO REFERENCE — NOT OFFICIAL BIS TEXT
WATER-RESIST (DEMO) — Water Contact, Sealing and Material Guidance for Kettles

IMPORTANT NOTICE:
This document is simulated solely for a prototype demonstration.
It is NOT official BIS text and must not be interpreted as verified BIS
material, legal guidance, or certification criteria.

Clause 1 — Scope
1.1 This fictional reference addresses example design considerations for
kettles that routinely contact water.

Clause 4.1 — Water-Contact Surfaces
4.1.1 Surfaces intended for repeated water contact shall be suitable for
their intended operating environment.

Clause 5.2 — Sealing
5.2.1 Seals and joints shall be arranged to reduce unintended water entry
into electrical compartments.

Clause 6.1 — Leakage
6.1.1 The appliance shall be designed to reduce the likelihood that normal
water handling results in contact between water and hazardous live parts.

Clause 7.4 — Materials
7.4.1 Materials used near heating components shall tolerate the simulated
temperature and moisture conditions expected during normal use.

Clause 9.2 — Inspection
9.2.1 Inspection shall check visible seals, joints, and water-contact surfaces
for damage before the appliance is placed into service.
