THERMAL-SAFE (DEMO) — Heating and Temperature-Control Guidance for Kettles

IMPORTANT NOTICE:
This is fictional demonstration content. It is NOT official BIS text and
does not represent a verified requirement, test method, or certification rule.

Clause 1 — Scope
1.1 This simulated document covers example thermal-safety clauses for
household electric kettles.

Clause 4.1 — Heating Element
4.1.1 The heating element shall be positioned to reduce unintended contact
with accessible parts.

Clause 5.2 — Temperature Control
5.2.1 The appliance shall include a control strategy that limits heating
under normal operating conditions.

Clause 6.1 — Surface Temperature
6.1.1 Accessible user-contact surfaces shall remain below the simulated
temperature-rise limit specified by the applicable general requirements.

Clause 7.3 — Thermal Cut-Off
7.3.1 A thermal protection device may be used to interrupt heating when an
abnormal temperature condition is detected.

Clause 8.2 — Repeated Operation
8.2.1 Repeated normal heating cycles shall not cause progressive degradation
that creates an unreasonable simulated safety risk.

Clause 9.1 — Cooling
9.1.1 The appliance shall be designed so that heat can dissipate after the
heating cycle without damaging normal-use components.
