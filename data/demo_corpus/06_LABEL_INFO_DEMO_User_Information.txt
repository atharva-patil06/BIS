SIMULATED / DEMO REFERENCE — NOT OFFICIAL BIS TEXT
LABEL-INFO (DEMO) — Marking, Instructions and User Information for Kettles

IMPORTANT NOTICE:
All clauses in this file are fictional demonstration data. This is NOT an
official BIS publication and must not be presented as verified BIS wording.

Clause 1 — Scope
1.1 This simulated reference gives example user-information requirements
for household electric kettles.

Clause 3.1 — Rating Label
3.1.1 The product label shall display the simulated rated voltage and power
input in a location visible during ordinary inspection.

Clause 4.2 — Manufacturer Information
4.2.1 Manufacturer or responsible-brand information shall be provided on the
appliance or its accompanying documentation.

Clause 5.1 — Operating Instructions
5.1.1 Instructions shall explain filling, switching on, pouring, and normal
shutdown procedures.

Clause 6.3 — Safety Warnings
6.3.1 Instructions shall warn users about hot surfaces, hot water, and steam.

Clause 7.1 — Maintenance
7.1.1 Instructions shall provide basic cleaning and maintenance guidance.

Clause 8.2 — Electrical Safety
8.2.1 Users shall be instructed not to operate an appliance with visibly
damaged electrical cords, plugs, or enclosures.
