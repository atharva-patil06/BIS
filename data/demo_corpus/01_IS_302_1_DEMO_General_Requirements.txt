SIMULATED / DEMO STANDARD — NOT OFFICIAL BIS TEXT
IS 302-1 (DEMO) — Household Electrical Appliances — Safety — Part 1: General Requirements

IMPORTANT NOTICE:
This document is entirely simulated for prototype demonstration purposes.
It is NOT an official Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) document and must
NOT be treated as verified BIS requirements.

Clause 1 — Scope
1.1 This simulated document gives example safety requirements for household
electrical appliances used under normal domestic conditions.

Clause 2 — Definitions
2.1 "Appliance" means the electrical product being evaluated.
2.2 "Accessible part" means a part that can reasonably be touched by a user.

Clause 5.1 — Marking
5.1.1 The appliance shall be marked with rated voltage, rated power input,
and the manufacturer's name or trademark.

Clause 7.2 — Insulation
7.2.1 Live parts shall be inaccessible during normal use.
7.2.2 Basic insulation shall withstand a simulated test voltage of 1250 V
for Class I appliances.

Clause 9.4 — Heating
9.4.1 Temperature rise of accessible surfaces during normal operation shall
not exceed 60 °C above ambient temperature.

Clause 11.3 — Earthing
11.3.1 Exposed conductive parts intended for protective connection shall
provide a reliable protective-earthing path.

Clause 13.2 — Power Connection
13.2.1 The supply connection shall be secured so that normal handling does
not place excessive mechanical stress on electrical connections.

Clause 15.1 — Abnormal Operation
15.1.1 The appliance shall be designed so that a foreseeable abnormal
condition does not create an unreasonable risk of electric shock or fire.

Clause 18.1 — Documentation
18.1.1 Instructions shall include basic electrical ratings, operating
precautions, and cleaning or maintenance guidance.
